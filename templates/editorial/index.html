Our thinking

Understanding
comes first.

Before a diagram. Before an interface. Before another feature. Find the idea that makes the whole thing make sense.

When people can explain a system, they can work on it together. Clear language turns individual knowledge into something a team can build on.

From idea
to shared practice.

Three useful moves when a technical problem feels bigger than it needs to be.

  1. 01

    Name the real problem.

    Describe what a person needs to understand or accomplish before describing the technology.

  2. 02

    Draw the boundaries.

    Give each part a clear responsibility. Show where information enters, changes, and leaves.

  3. 03

    Make it explainable.

    Use familiar words and concrete examples. Leave a path for the next person to follow.

Keep asking better questions.
