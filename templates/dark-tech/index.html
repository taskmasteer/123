Architecture

A boundary you
can reason about.

Each participant follows explicit rules. Inputs have a shape, transitions have conditions, and results carry enough context to understand what happened.

  1. Define the intent

    State the operation and the information needed to evaluate it.

  2. Apply the shared rules

    Check the conditions before a state transition can proceed.

  3. Leave a readable record

    Make the result available for inspection by the next participant.
